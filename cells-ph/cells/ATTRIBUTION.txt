Changes made by Los Radios: only Philippine networks (MCC 515) are kept; rows with an unknown radio type, unusable
coordinates or a position outside the Philippines are removed; where the same cell appears more than once only the
most recently updated row is kept; the averageSignal and changeable columns are dropped; the rows are re-encoded in
the binary .cel v1 format, sorted by network and latitude. No positions or values were altered.
The "range" value is OpenCelliD's estimate of how far a tower's position may be off. It is not coverage, and the app
never uses it as coverage.
